# Privacy Policy for Remix

Last updated: October 2026

Remix respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. 

## Data We Collect
- **Discord IDs & Guild Information:** Used strictly for functionality like music queue management, voice channel permissions, and user preferences.
- **Message Content / Command Inputs:** Processed in real-time to handle bot commands and play audio links. We do not store or log message contents off-platform.

## Data Storage & Security
All collected data is kept secure. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to outside parties.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our GitHub repository: https://github.com/remix-bot/discord
